ENTERPRISE HR POLICY DOCUMENT

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

Welcome to Enterprise Solutions Pvt. Ltd. This document outlines the Human Resource policies, employee guidelines, workplace expectations, and benefits available to all employees. Every employee is expected to read and follow these policies throughout their employment.

2. OFFICE WORKING HOURS

The standard office working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to be present during core working hours. Flexible working hours may be approved by department managers based on business requirements.

Employees must mark attendance daily through the company attendance system.

Late arrivals exceeding three times in a month may result in a warning from the reporting manager.

3. WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Work-from-home requests should be submitted at least one day in advance unless there is an emergency.

Employees working remotely must remain available during official working hours and attend all scheduled meetings.

The company reserves the right to revoke remote work privileges if productivity is affected.

4. LEAVE POLICY

Every full-time employee receives 24 paid leaves per calendar year.

Leave types include:

Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per year.

Sick Leave (SL): 8 days per year.

Personal Leave (PL): 4 days per year.

Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

Employees must apply for planned leave at least 3 working days in advance.

Emergency leave requests should be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as possible.

5. MATERNITY LEAVE POLICY

Eligible female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.

Additional unpaid leave may be granted subject to management approval.

Medical documentation may be required before approval.

6. PATERNITY LEAVE POLICY

Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave following the birth of a child.

The leave must be used within six months of the child's birth.

7. NOTICE PERIOD POLICY

Employees resigning from the organization must serve a notice period of 60 days.

Management may approve a shorter notice period under exceptional circumstances.

Failure to serve the notice period may result in salary deductions as per company policy.

8. DRESS CODE POLICY

Employees are expected to maintain professional and appropriate attire.

Business casual clothing is recommended.

Clothing containing offensive language, inappropriate graphics, or unprofessional content is prohibited.

Formal attire is required during client meetings and company events.

9. CODE OF CONDUCT

All employees must demonstrate professionalism, integrity, and respect.

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and workplace violence are strictly prohibited.

Employees must maintain confidentiality of company information and client data.

Any violation of company policies may result in disciplinary action.

10. PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY

Employee performance reviews are conducted twice every year.

Performance evaluations consider:

Work quality

Productivity

Team collaboration

Attendance

Communication skills

Employees demonstrating exceptional performance may be eligible for promotions and bonuses.

11. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

The company provides the following benefits:

Health insurance coverage

Annual performance bonus

Paid vacation leave

Training and development programs

Professional certification reimbursement

Employee wellness programs

12. INTERNET AND EMAIL USAGE

Company internet and email systems are intended for business purposes.

Employees must not access unauthorized, illegal, or harmful content using company resources.

Monitoring may be performed to ensure compliance with company policies.

13. INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Employees must protect confidential company information.

Passwords should not be shared with anyone.

Company devices should be locked when unattended.

Any suspected security incident must be reported immediately to the IT department.

14. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

The company encourages continuous learning.

Employees may participate in internal and external training programs.

Managers should support professional growth and skill development.

15. TRAVEL POLICY

Business travel must be approved by the reporting manager.

Reasonable travel, accommodation, and meal expenses will be reimbursed according to company guidelines.

Receipts must be submitted within 15 days of travel completion.

16. TERMINATION POLICY

Employment may be terminated due to:

Resignation

Retirement

Performance issues

Policy violations

Business restructuring

Employees must return all company assets upon separation.

17. HEALTH AND SAFETY

Employees must follow workplace safety guidelines at all times.

Unsafe behavior should be reported immediately.

Emergency exits and safety equipment should remain unobstructed.

18. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY POLICY

The company provides equal employment opportunities regardless of gender, religion, race, nationality, disability, or age.

All employment decisions are based on qualifications, performance, and business needs.

19. GRIEVANCE POLICY

Employees may raise workplace concerns through their manager or HR department.

All complaints will be handled confidentially and investigated fairly.

Retaliation against employees who report concerns is strictly prohibited.

20. POLICY ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

All employees are required to acknowledge that they have read and understood this HR Policy Document.

Failure to comply with company policies may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment.
